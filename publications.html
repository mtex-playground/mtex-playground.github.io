    The papers behind MTEX

    If MTEX was part of your research, please cite the paper that describes the method you used. Most of them come with the MTEX scripts that draw their figures.

    Citing MTEX in general

    F. Bachmann, R. Hielscher, H. Schaeben: Texture Analysis with MTEX - Free and Open Source Software Toolbox. Solid State Phenomena 160, 63-68 (2010). doi:10.4028/www.scientific.net/SSP.160.63
