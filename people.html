    People

    The people behind MTEX

    Mathematicians, materials scientists and geologists, many of whom started as users. The photo shows the 2024 workshop in Freiberg.

    Ralf Hielscher

    Founder and lead developer

    Invented MTEX in 2007 by implementing the algorithm that turns pole figures into an ODF, and has led its development since then.

    Institute of Applied Analysis, TU Bergakademie Freiberg

    Rüdiger Kilian

    EBSD and grains

    Joined MTEX in 2008 as a PhD student of Renée Heilbronner in Basel. He has answered hundreds of questions in the forum and keeps improving the EBSD and grain analysis of MTEX.

    Martin-Luther-Universität Halle-Wittenberg

    David Mainprice

    Tensors and seismic properties

    Joined MTEX in 2009 by sharing his code for tensor properties and seismic velocities, and has brought MTEX to the geoscience community ever since.

    Géosciences Montpellier, France

    Luiz Fernando Grafulha Morales

    Physical properties, support and workshops

    A structural geologist, electron microscopist and long-time MTEX user. He helps with physical properties, user support and the workshops.

    ScopeM, ETH Zürich, Switzerland

    Florian Bachmann

    Grain reconstruction

    Started with MTEX in his diploma thesis at TU Freiberg in 2008, where he implemented the grain reconstruction. He also built the import wizard and the documentation centre.

    Xnovo Technology ApS, Denmark

    Bjørn Eske Sørensen

    Spectral diffraction and birefringence

    Contributes to spectral diffraction and birefringence.

    Department of Geoscience and Petroleum, NTNU, Norway

    Tuomo Nyyssönen

    Parent grain reconstruction

    Worked on parent austenite reconstruction during his PhD at Tampere University of Technology, and wrote the first MTEX code for reconstructing parent grains and determining the orientation relationship.

    Frank Niessen

    Orientation relationships

    Generalised the orientation relationship analysis and parent grain reconstruction to arbitrary phase combinations, and wrote the export to ctf and ang files.

    Department of Mechanical Engineering, Technical University of Denmark

    Vivian Tong

    TrueEBSD

    Uses MTEX to study plastic deformation in engineering materials. She developed TrueEBSD, the image registration for correlative microscopy, and contributed many features and fixes.

    National Physical Laboratory, UK

    Azdiar Gazder

    Orientation relationships

    Worked on the orientation relationship analysis and parent grain reconstruction for arbitrary phase combinations. Co-developer of the ORTools and phaseSegmenter toolboxes and author of mtexTools.

    Electron Microscopy Centre, University of Wollongong

    Felix Bartel

    Spherical functions

    Wrote the classes of spherical functions during his master's thesis at TU Chemnitz in 2019.

    Institute of Mathematics, TU Chemnitz

    Erik Wünsche

    Functions on the rotation group

    Wrote the classes of functions on the rotation group during his PhD at TU Freiberg.

    TU Bergakademie Freiberg

    Oliver Kent Johnson

    Misorientation colour key

    Implemented the misorientation colour key. He finished his PhD in 2015 with Christopher A. Schuh at MIT.

    Fabian Bartl

    Website

    Has looked after the website since 2021.

    TU Bergakademie Freiberg
